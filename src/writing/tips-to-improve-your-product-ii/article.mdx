# Tips to improve your product II

## Number inputs change when you scroll

A focused number input quietly changes its value when you scroll over it, so people edit amounts without noticing. Click an amount, then scroll the checkbook with the cursor still over it.

<NumberInputScroll />

```html
<!-- Before -->
<input type="number" />

<!-- After -->
<input type="text" inputmode="decimal" />
```

<br />

## Don't reorder a list under the cursor

Lists that sort themselves live, like who spoke last, move rows away right as you go to click them. Try to mute Jonas while he keeps talking.

<br />

## Don't disable the submit button

A disabled button never tells you what's missing, it just ignores the click. Click _Invite_ with the address as it is and compare both modes.

## Escape closes only the top layer

Escape should close whatever sits on top, not everything behind it too. Open the Repeat menu, then press Esc and see what stays open.

## Select the value when a field is clicked

When a field is clicked, people usually want to replace the value, not edit it. Click into W and type 320.

## Select a range with shift-click

In a list with checkboxes, people expect shift-click to select everything in between, like in Finder or Gmail. Check Studio rent, then hold shift and click Team lunch.

<ShiftClickRange />

<br />

## Drag things from where they were grabbed

Whatever you pick up should stay under the cursor at the spot you grabbed it. Many drags center the item on the cursor instead, so it leaps on the first frame. Grab a card by its corner in both modes. The same goes for slider thumbs and scrollbars.

<DragOffset />

<br />

## Give colors their own dark mode values

A color picked on a white page rarely survives a dark one. These notes were colored for light mode, and in dark mode they glare like light boxes. Switch to light to see them as they were picked. Keep the hue, but give dark mode its own value, a little dimmer and more saturated.
